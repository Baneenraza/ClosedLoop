# Loop — Full Pitch Script (All Slides)

Simple, punchy spoken script for the whole deck. For the deeper technical Q&A reference (engine/model breakdown, hard-question cheat sheet), see `PITCH_SCRIPT.md`.

---

**[Slide 1 — Title]**

Loop. Your digital watchman.
A proactive shield against scams, deepfakes, and fraud — built for the people who don't even know to be suspicious yet.

**[Slide 2 — Core Idea]**

Most safety apps wait for you to get suspicious before they help. Loop doesn't wait. It sits on your phone and steps between you and the danger — before the link opens, before the message gets acted on.

*"We check the door you're about to walk through. We don't read your house."*

Two ways it does that: a floating shield bubble that lives over every app, and real link interception — Loop is your default browser, so any link you tap gets checked first.

**[Slide 3 — Check Engine]**

One engine. Three inputs — text, links, photos. One output — a color, one sentence, spoken out loud, in English or Urdu.

Red: stop. Yellow: be careful. Green: looks safe.

Groq classifies text. Gemini reads images. And screenshots? We don't even try — *"screenshots prove nothing, check your own app."* Because pretending to analyze a screenshot would just be lying more convincingly.

**[Slide 4 — Target Audience]**

We're not building for people who already suspect a scam. We're building for the people who wouldn't think to check at all — low-literacy, non-technical, Urdu-speaking Android users in Pakistan. The exact people every English-only security app leaves behind.

**[Slide 5 — Why It Works]**

Voice and color instead of paragraphs. Proactive instead of opt-in. Bilingual, everywhere, all the time.

The barrier isn't the app being hard to use — it's the user never opening it in the first place. So we made it open itself.

**[Slide 6 — Credibility]**

We'll tell you what doesn't work before you ask.

Everything here — text check, image check, link interception, the bubble — real, running, right now. VERIFY and FLAG are roleplay for today's demo, and we're saying so.

Here's the part that matters: nobody can reliably detect a deepfake in real time. We didn't just say that — we tested it. Two different deepfake models, two real deepfakes — each model caught one, missed the other.

That's not bad luck. That's proof the problem doesn't go away with a better model — it just moves. So Loop defends the decision, not the pixel: hang up, call back, ask for the code word. That beats even a perfect fake.

**[Slide 7 — Implementation]**

Why Android? Link-handling, overlays, share-targets — that's real OS-level access iOS doesn't give you. Not a convenience. The differentiator.

Kotlin on the front line. Node backend, zero dependencies. Groq and Gemini doing the thinking.

**[Slide 8 — Proof]**

Every screenshot here is the real app on a real phone. Not mockups. This just happened.

**[Slide 9 — Close]**

A shield that checks the door, not the house.
Proactive. Bilingual. Honest about what it can't do yet.
Loop — your digital watchman.
